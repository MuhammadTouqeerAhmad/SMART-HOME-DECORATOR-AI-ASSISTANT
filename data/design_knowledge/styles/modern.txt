Modern Interior Design

Modern interiors commonly emphasize clean lines,
functional furniture and a balanced visual appearance.

Common characteristics include:

- Clean geometric shapes
- Simple furniture
- Neutral base colors
- Controlled accent colors
- Functional lighting
- Open visual space
- Limited unnecessary decoration

Modern designs should avoid overcrowding a room.

Furniture should be arranged to maintain comfortable
movement through the room.

Lighting can be used as both a functional and
decorative element.
