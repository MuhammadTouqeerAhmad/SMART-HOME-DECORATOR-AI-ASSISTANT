Minimalist Interior Design

Minimalist interiors focus on functionality,
simplicity and visual clarity.

Important characteristics:

- Neutral colors
- Functional furniture
- Limited decoration
- Clear walking paths
- Organized storage
- Simple shapes
- Natural light
- Uncluttered surfaces

Small rooms should avoid unnecessary furniture.

Vertical storage can provide additional storage
while consuming less floor space.

Existing furniture should be retained when it is
functional and compatible with the requested design.
