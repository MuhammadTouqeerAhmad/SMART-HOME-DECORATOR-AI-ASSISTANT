Furniture Placement

Furniture placement should prioritize:

1. Safety
2. Comfortable movement
3. Functionality
4. Visual balance

Do not block:

- Doors
- Windows
- Main walking paths
- Heating or cooling equipment

Large furniture should generally be placed where it
does not unnecessarily reduce circulation space.

Existing functional furniture should be retained whenever
possible.

Room dimensions should be considered before recommending
additional furniture.
